# E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard built on 5,000 synthetic e-commerce orders to track revenue, orders, delivery time and customer ratings.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle – E-Commerce Sales Analytics
- 5,000 rows, 12 columns
- Columns: order_id, order_date, customer_id, product_category, region, quantity, unit_price, discount, payment_method, delivery_days, customer_rating, revenue

## Visuals
- Bar chart: Revenue by Product Category
- Column chart: Revenue by Region
- Donut chart: Revenue by Payment Method
- Line chart: Revenue Trend Over Time
- Donut chart: Orders by Delivery Status
- Column chart: Rating by Category
- Bar chart: Revenue by Discount Category
- Column chart: Orders by Delivery Days

## Key Findings
- 5.11M revenue from 5K orders and 20K items sold
- Average order value is about 1.02K
- Average delivery time is 6.12 days
- Revenue can be viewed by category, region, payment method, discount band and time
